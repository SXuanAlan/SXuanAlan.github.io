Shixuan (Alan) Zhao

I am an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at Stanford University, where I am a graduate research assistant in the Biomimetics & Dexterous Manipulation Lab advised by Mark Cutkosky, and work with C. Karen Liu on humanoid control.

I received dual bachelor's degrees in Mechanical Engineering from The University of Arizona and Hebei University of Technology.

My interests are in humanoid whole-body control and robot learning, especially contact-rich behaviors where the robot has to use its whole body, not just its feet, to interact with the world.

Humanoid Wall Climbing with Compliant Spines In progress

Shixuan Zhao, Sam Xie, Hao Li, Hayi Zhao, Justin Chen, Teo Ren, Sirui Chen, Zhanpeng He, Qi Wu, C. Karen Liu, Mark R. Cutkosky

In preparation

webpage (coming soon)

Teaching a humanoid to climb steep walls with micro-spine end-effectors: a physics-based spine contact model in simulation, RL policies that track contact-feasible whole-body references, proprioceptive adaptation in place of force sensing, and deployment on hardware.

Fluorescence-Enhanced Whisker Array with Vision-Based Deformation Analysis for Underwater Source Localization

Xiaochi Xie*, Hao Li*, Shixuan Zhao, Siyue Yao, Shuran Song, Mark R. Cutkosky

Submitted to ICRA 2027

webpage| pdf

Five glowing whiskers and a single camera locate an underwater flow source in the dark. I built the lightweight CNN that decodes whisker deformation into source position.

Lego-Like Stiffness Configuration of Planar Compliant Modules for Task-Specific Flexible Interfaces

Siyue Yao, Xiaochi Xie, Shixuan Zhao, Yutong Li, Hao Li, Mark R. Cutkosky, Genliang Chen

Submitted to ICRA 2027

webpage| pdf

Stacking a few planar compliant modules gives a robot wrist the stiffness a task needs. I implemented the robot control for the contact and high-speed interaction experiments.
